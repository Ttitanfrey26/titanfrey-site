Titan Frey is a former NBA journalist with more than six years of experience covering the league. He served as editor-in-chief of FadeawayWorld.net, a basketball publication that operated under the Sports Illustrated media umbrella. He has also written for other sports websites, including BallExclusives.com, where he continued developing his distinctive approach to modern basketball storytelling.

Titan is also an accomplished multi-genre creator. He is the author of more than ten published books and a screenwriter with three produced short films. His work extends into gaming and digital media, including co-writing a video game and crafting scripts for clients such as Oldskoolbball, a YouTube channel known for collaborations with NBA.com, ESPN, and The Last Dance documentary.

He also creates content for his own basketball-focused YouTube channel, where he explores the history, culture, and evolution of the sport.

Titan is also a living kidney donor, a deeply personal chapter of his life that informs the humanity and emotional depth found throughout his work.
